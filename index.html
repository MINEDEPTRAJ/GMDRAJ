Why chokho

A stepwell doesn't chase the water. It waits, low and patient, until the water is worth drinking.

Rajasthan's baoris were built centuries before pipelines — stepped, shaded, sealed against the sand above. CHOKHOPANI follows the same instinct: draw from a protected source, filter in eleven stages, and seal it before the desert gets a chance to touch it.

No shortcuts between the source and the seal. What leaves our plant is what reaches you.
